Our Mission

Why VidyaUday Exists

Thousands of bright rural students across Andhra Pradesh miss life-changing opportunities every year — not because of lack of talent, but because of lack of information. A scholarship deadline passes. A free course goes unused. An AI tool that could help with studies is never discovered.

VidyaUday was built by students, for students — to be the guide that every rural learner deserves but rarely gets.
